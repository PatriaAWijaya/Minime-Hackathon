# Naskah Demo Livestream — Minime-Hackathon (3 menit)

Aturan: HANYA tampilkan pekerjaan 27 Okt–4 Nov 2026. Naskah ini ditulis
untuk dibawakan live, dan narasinya bisa langsung dipakai untuk video
demo 3 menit. Narasi dalam bahasa Inggris (untuk juri); aba-aba panggung
dalam bahasa Indonesia (untuk presenter).

Perkiraan durasi: 435 kata narasi = 3:00 pada tempo demo 145 kata/menit
(dihitung per segmen; revisi 4 Nov 19:xx — naskah lama hanya 221 kata
≈ 1:31, terlalu pendek untuk 3 menit).

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook + masalah

[Aba-aba: tampilkan dashboard Minime, satu project kosong. Jangan slide.]

Narasi:
"In Indonesia, important work often begins with a short message. The
problem is not generating text. It is turning one incomplete brief into
coordinated, accountable teamwork — every task owned, every step
approved by a human. Minime does that: one person, five AI teammates,
all supervised by a human."

## 0:20–0:50 — Brief masuk, tugas terbelah

[Aba-aba: ketik satu brief singkat di chat, kirim. Tunjukkan bubble
user + ack Prime langsung tampil, lalu tugas-tugas terbelah ke Kanban.]

Narasi:
"I type one brief — one product, one week of content. Watch what
happens. Minime splits it into owned tasks: research, strategy,
creative, finance, and code. You see my message appear, then Prime
answers immediately, and the tasks land on the board — each with an
owner, each with a status. Nothing is anonymous work. Nothing
disappears into a chat log. If a task stalls, I see where it stalled,
and who owns it."

## 0:50–1:30 — Agen bekerja, artefak berpindah

[Aba-aba: pindah ke Graph — tunjukkan node agen bekerja; buka Library —
tunjukkan artefak yang sudah lewat antar agen: riset, STP, brief.]

Narasi:
"Each agent works in the background — not one chat answering
everything, but a team passing work along. Research hands its findings
to strategy. Strategy hands its brief to creative. Creative hands its
draft back for review. On the graph you can see the team: nodes
working, edges carrying artifacts from one owner to the next. In the
library, the proof is there: research notes, a positioning brief, a
draft — each produced by an agent, each traceable to the brief it came
from. Artifacts pass between agents, and a human approves the result.
One reliable loop — not a feature demo."

## 1:30–2:10 — Planner: draft-only, tanpa auto-post

[Aba-aba: buka Planner — tunjukkan draf yang sudah dibuat dari brief,
pengingat H-3, dan dialog konfirmasi terbit. Tekankan: postingan tunggal
tidak pernah terbit otomatis.]

Narasi:
"Publishing is draft-only by default. A single post never goes out on
its own. It waits for my approval, with a reminder three days before it
is due. Look at the planner: drafts made from this same brief, each
with its date, each waiting — not sent. When I open one, I can edit it,
reschedule it, or delete it. And when I do approve, a confirmation
dialog asks me once more. Scheduled batches only publish after I
approve the whole batch. Nothing here publishes by accident. Safety is
not a setting here. It is the design."

## 2:10–2:45 — QC Prime + Media Studio

[Aba-aba: tunjukkan satu hasil yang lewat QC Prime (maksimal 2 revisi),
lalu Media Studio — voice/TTS atau gambar hasil generate.]

Narasi:
"Every creative output passes Prime quality control. A different model
family checks the work — the maker never grades itself — with at most
two revisions. If it fails twice, it comes back to me, not to an
endless loop. Here is one result that passed: you can see the revision,
and the approval. And the studio turns approved work into voice and
visuals — a voice-over from the approved script, an image from the
approved prompt. Same brief, same approval, one loop from words to
media."

## 2:45–3:00 — Metrik + visi + repo

[Aba-aba: tampilkan footer Total …ms / biaya per tugas bila ada, lalu
tampilkan URL repo di layar. Kalimat penutup langsung ke kamera.]

Narasi:
"Minime brings Meta's newest models into the workflow one person runs.
The result is not activity for its own sake. It is visible, traceable
teamwork that a solo founder can supervise. Repo on screen. Thank you."

## Catatan live (bila dibawakan langsung, bukan rekaman)

- Aplikasi harus sudah berjalan sebelum mulai; jangan buka slide.
- Bila panggilan model live melambat: JANGAN dipalsukan. Potong ke klip
  rekaman proses yang sama, atau tunjukkan hasil yang sudah selesai di
  Library — lalu lanjutkan.
- Siapkan satu project dengan brief + hasil yang sudah jadi sebagai
  cadangan, supaya demo tidak bergantung penuh pada panggilan live.
- Akhiri dengan URL repo tampil di layar minimal 5 detik.
