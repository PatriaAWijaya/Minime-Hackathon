Minime — one person, five AI teammates, all supervised by a human.

Minime is an agent orchestrator demo built from scratch for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026 (Indie track, Build for Your World). Type one brief, and Minime splits it into owned tasks across a small team of AI agents — research, strategy, creative, finance, and code. Agents pass artifacts to each other, a different model family checks the work (Prime QC, max 2 revisions), and a human approves the result. Publishing is draft-only by default: a single post never goes out on its own.

In this 3-minute demo:
0:00 The problem: turning one incomplete brief into accountable teamwork
0:20 One brief in, owned tasks out on the Kanban board
0:50 Agents at work: the Graph and the Library
1:30 Planner: draft-only publishing, H-3 reminders, batch approval
2:10 Prime QC and the Media Studio
2:45 Live metrics, and the repo

Built with Meta's Muse Spark models via the Meta Model API, plus Gemini and local Ollama models. Every creative output is checked by a different model family than the one that made it, and nothing consequential ships without human approval.

Built from scratch during the hackathon window (October 27 – November 4, 2026). The demo shows only work built during the event.
